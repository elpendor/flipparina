# Flipparina

Plays the ocarina in **Zelda: Ocarina of Time** and **Majora's Mask** on the Nintendo Switch. The Flipper acts as a wired USB pad and presses the buttons for you, or plays the notes out loud for the Switch 2 version's microphone.

## Features

- All official ocarina songs from both games, with the games' own timing
- MIDI player for the melody of any .mid file in apps_data/flipparina, with automatic key, tempo and channel controls
- Outputs: **N64 (NSO)** pad, **Switch 2** pad, or the Flipper **speaker** for the Switch 2 microphone
- Asks whether the ocarina is out and takes it out for you
- **Take control** makes the Flipper the active controller

## How to use

- Pick an **Output** in **Settings** and plug the Flipper into the Switch
- Pick a song and press **OK**
- Up/Down: previous/next song or file; Left/Right: tempo
- MIDI files: hold Up/Down for key, hold Left/Right for channel
